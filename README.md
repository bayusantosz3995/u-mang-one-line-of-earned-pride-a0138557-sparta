A single line of earned pride: after eleven months of 5 a.m. drills, Raka
reads his name on the pass list — 87th of 412 — and walks home slower
than he ever has. A small person, a long effort, finally landing.

`main.py` writes the line to `entry.txt` (1–240 characters, single line)
and verifies it byte-for-byte.
